work_outline KEIKO System Architecture • Module 02

Job Description Intelligence & Role Profile Synthesis

The Job Description Intelligence Module extracts technical requirements, domain responsibilities, and expected experience levels from target job postings. It audits JDs for bias and inclusivity, benchmarks market alignment, and produces a structured Role Profile.

PROCESS 01

Requirement Extraction

Categorizes required skills into Must-Have core tech vs. Nice-to-Have secondary tools, minimum experience years, and degree benchmarks.

PROCESS 02

Inclusivity & Bias Audit

Scans job postings for gender-coded words, exclusionary requirements, or unrealistic skill demands to ensure fair and inclusive candidate attraction.

PROCESS 03

Market Competitiveness

Benchmarks compensation ranges, seniority expectations, and skill rarity against real-world engineering market data.

PROCESS 04

Interview Target Planning

Feeds the Role Profile to the Question Generator agent to dictate key evaluation topics, scenario difficulty, and problem-solving focus areas.

menu_book Academic Rationale (KEIKO Report Section IV.2)

As outlined in Section IV.2 of the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) report, analyzing job descriptions creates an objective baseline for candidate evaluation. By extracting core domain competencies and matching them against candidate resumes, KEIKO eliminates generic question templates and focuses on precise role requirements.
