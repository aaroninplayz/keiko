Central Evaluation Engine & Multi-Agent Intelligence Platform

The Central Evaluation Engine is the core decision-making orchestration layer of KEIKO. It coordinates Resume Intelligence, JD Intelligence, Video Kinetics, Audio Processing, and LLMs into an adaptive multi-agent closed loop to deliver candidate assessments across 8 core evaluation dimensions.

grid_view The 8 Multidimensional Candidate Assessment Categories

DIMENSION 01

Technical Competency

Subject knowledge, problem-solving, architectural depth, code clarity, and practical skill verification.

DIMENSION 02

Communication Skills

Speech clarity, structured answers, vocabulary richness, WPM rate, and idea articulation.

DIMENSION 03

Behavioural Assessment

Work ethic, teamwork, conflict resolution, accountability, leadership potential, and decision-making.

DIMENSION 04

Psychological Indicators

Vocal confidence, composure under pressure, stress management, and emotional resilience during difficulty ramps.

DIMENSION 05

Professional Presence

Posture alignment, eye contact consistency, facial engagement, attentiveness, and overall interview poise.

DIMENSION 06

Cultural & Organizational Fit

Alignment with company values, workplace preferences, motivation, and career expectations.

DIMENSION 07

Learning Potential

Curiosity, adaptability to unfamiliar technical scenarios, openness to feedback, and growth mindset.

DIMENSION 08

Role Alignment

Direct match between candidate profile capabilities and target job posting expectations.
