Resume Intelligence & Candidate Profile Normalization

The Resume Intelligence Module serves as the initial stage of candidate understanding in KEIKO. It uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to parse unstructured CVs (PDF/DOCX), extract technical taxonomies, evaluate project depth, and build a normalized Candidate Profile that powers dynamic interview question generation.

account_tree How Resume Intelligence Processes Candidate Data

STAGE 01

Document Ingestion & Text Extraction

Processes uploaded PDF, DOCX, or plain text resumes. Cleans whitespace, strips formatting noise, and extracts raw textual tokens into a structured document DOM.

STAGE 02

Named Entity & Skill Taxonomy Mining

Scans text using specialized NLP entities to extract programming languages, frameworks, cloud platforms, databases, certifications, and academic degrees.

STAGE 03

Proficiency & Experience Depth Estimation

Analyzes project descriptions, job titles, and tenure duration to estimate technical career level (Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead) and practical technology depth.

STAGE 04

Resume–JD Alignment & Skill Gap Analysis

Compares normalized candidate profile against target job description requirements to calculate alignment percentage and pinpoint missing stack skills.

menu_book Academic & Technical Rationale

As detailed in Section IV.1 of the KEIKO Report, traditional keyword matching in resumes fails to capture context, domain complexity, or practical proficiency. KEIKO's Resume Intelligence analyzes project descriptions, frameworks used, and responsibilities to formulate a structured profile that feeds directly into the Interview Intelligence Engine.

hub Role Alignment Engine

By performing automated alignment scoring (Section IV.3), KEIKO identifies where a candidate's background matches job expectations and where gaps exist. Rather than rejecting candidates with gaps, the system dynamically schedules targeted interview questions to test learning adaptability.
