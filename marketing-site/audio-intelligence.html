Audio Intelligence & Speech Pattern Processing

The Audio Intelligence Module captures verbal communication during interviews. While the LLM evaluates response content, this module evaluates *how* responses are delivered: speaking pace (WPM), voice modulation, articulation clarity, and filler word frequency.

PILLAR 01

Whisper Speech-to-Text Pipeline

Converts live candidate speech into real-time text streams, generating precise transcripts that enable LLMs to analyze technical answer correctness.

PILLAR 02

Voice Modulation & Pitch Analysis

Measures pitch variation, speaking energy, and vocal confidence to identify expressive, engaging delivery versus monotone or hesitant speech.

PILLAR 03

Speaking Rate & Pace (WPM)

Calculates Words Per Minute (WPM) speed against ideal benchmarks (130-150 WPM), identifying rushed or excessively slow response patterns.

PILLAR 04

Filler Word & Fluency Tracking

Detects hesitation markers (*um, uh, like, you know*) and long pauses, quantifying speech fluency and articulation clarity.
