videocam KEIKO System Architecture • Module 03

Video Intelligence & Visual Kinetic Telemetry

The Video Intelligence Module processes real-time video frames from the webcam feed using Computer Vision and MediaPipe. It measures facial expressions, eye contact consistency, spine posture alignment, and non-verbal professional presence throughout the interview session.

PILLAR 01

Facial Expression Analysis

Uses MediaPipe facial mesh blendshapes to track 468 facial landmarks, evaluating confidence, neutral composure, enthusiasm, and signs of stress or uncertainty.

PILLAR 02

Eye Contact & Gaze Direction

Calculates iris displacement relative to screen center coordinates, measuring eye contact percentage and detecting visual distractions.

PILLAR 03

Posture & Spine Alignment

Monitors upper body posture and shoulder alignment angles over time to detect upright posture versus slouching or physical fatigue.

PILLAR 04

Professional Presence Synthesis

Synthesizes visual cues into a composite Professional Presence score (0-100) that is forwarded to the Evaluation Engine.

LIVE COMPUTER VISION SIMULATOR

Real-Time Facial Landmark & Posture Analyzer

Simulate live webcam kinetic telemetry processing using MediaPipe facial mesh and iris gaze tracking.

menu_book Academic Rationale (KEIKO Report Section VI)

Section VI of the KEIKO report specifies that visual assessment does not make arbitrary psychological claims. Instead, it measures observable, quantifiable indicators—such as posture stability and eye contact consistency—over time to produce an objective, balanced measure of professional presentation.
